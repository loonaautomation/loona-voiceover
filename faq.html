Loona Voiceover — Frequently Asked Questions

Loona Voiceover · Loona Automation

Is Loona Voiceover free?

Yes — completely. Type a script, hear it spoken, record your own voice and download the take, all with no sign-up and no payment. The studio runs in your browser, supported by Loona Automation.

Why don't I see an Urdu voice in the list?

Because the voices belong to your device, not to this website. The studio lists whatever voices your phone or computer has installed. English voices are on almost every device; an Urdu voice appears only if your device has one. Add Urdu in your device's language or text-to-speech settings and it will appear here after a refresh.

Can I download the spoken voiceover as an audio file?

The text-to-speech playback itself cannot be downloaded — browsers do not allow a page to record its own speech output. That is why the studio includes a recorder: record your own voice reading the script and every take can be downloaded as a file.

Is my script uploaded anywhere?

No. The speech is generated on your device by its own speech engine, and scripts you save are stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing you type is sent to a server to be read aloud.

Why does the same script sound different on another phone?

Because each device supplies its own voices. A script read by one phone's Urdu voice and another phone's Urdu voice is being read by two literally different voices, with different quality and character. The words, speed and pitch settings are the same; the voice is the device's.

How long can my script be?

Paste as much as you need — the studio splits your text into lines and reads them in order, and the counter estimates the total reading time before you start. On some browsers a very long session may pause partway; pressing Play All again continues the reading.

My recording will not start. What is wrong?

Almost always microphone permission. The browser asks for permission the first time; if it was denied, the recorder stays blocked until you allow the microphone for this site in your browser's settings. No recording is ever uploaded — takes stay on your device and download only when you tap Download.

Can I use the output in my videos and ads?

Your own recordings are yours — use them anywhere. For the device voices, the voice belongs to your device's maker, so for commercial work check that maker's terms; for lessons, drafts and everyday posts, the studio is used by teachers and small businesses without issue.
