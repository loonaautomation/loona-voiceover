We read every message and usually reply within 1–2 working days. Please mention Loona Voiceover in your subject line so we can help you faster.

What to write to us about

"I can't hear an Urdu voice." This is the most common message, and the answer is almost always the device, not the site: the studio can only use voices your phone or computer already has. Tell us your device model and browser, and we will reply with the exact settings path for adding an Urdu voice on it. If your device offers no Urdu voice at all, we will tell you that honestly too.

Recording and playback problems. If the recorder will not start, the usual cause is microphone permission — the browser asks once, and a denied permission stays denied until it is changed in the browser's site settings. Tell us what you see on screen (the exact message helps) and which browser you are using.

Scripts and teaching use. Teachers: if you use the studio for lesson audio and there is a sample script shape that would help your class — a dictation format, a poem layout, a question-and-answer pattern — write and suggest it. The sample list grows from teachers' requests.

Feature ideas. New sample scripts, better line controls, anything that would make the studio more useful for ads or lessons — all welcome.

Please do not send

Never email us passwords or one-time codes, and please do not send recordings of other people or scripts containing anyone's private information — a short sample with names changed is always enough for us to reproduce a problem.
