About Loona Voiceover

Loona Voiceover · Loona Automation

Loona Voiceover is a free text-to-speech studio that runs entirely in your browser. Type or paste a script, choose a voice, and hear it read aloud in Urdu or English — line by line or all at once — with speed and pitch under your control. When you would rather use your own voice, the built-in recorder captures your take and lets you download it as a file. No sign-up, no upload, no cost.

What it does

The Script Studio counts your words, characters and lines and estimates the reading time as you type, so you know before recording whether a script fits a 30-second ad or a five-minute lesson. Ready samples — an Urdu ad, an English ad, a lesson introduction — show the shape of a good script. Every line of your script becomes its own playable row: tap any line to hear just that line, or press Play All and the studio reads the script end to end, with pause and stop whenever you need them. Scripts you save are kept in this browser, ready next time, with no account and no internet needed to reopen them.

How it works — honestly

The voices come from your own device. Loona Voiceover uses the speech engine built into your phone or computer (the Web Speech API), which means two honest consequences. First, the voice list is your device's voice list: English voices exist on virtually every device, while an Urdu voice appears only if your device has one installed — if Urdu is missing, adding it in your device's language or text-to-speech settings is the fix, because no website can install a voice for you. Second, everything happens on the device: your script is not uploaded to a server to be spoken, and your saved scripts never leave this browser. The recorder works the same way — it uses your microphone, with your permission, on your device.

What it cannot do

A browser is not allowed to capture its own spoken audio as a downloadable file, so the text-to-speech playback cannot be saved as an MP3 from this page — that is a browser rule, not a missing button, and it is why the recorder exists: record your own voice and every take can be downloaded. Voice quality also depends entirely on your device's voices; a flagship phone's Urdu voice and an old phone's Urdu voice will not sound alike, because they are literally different voices.

Who it is for

Teachers making lesson introductions and audio for students who learn by listening; small businesses that need a spoken line for an ad today, not after hiring a voice artist; and creators drafting narration before committing to a final recording. It is a working studio for quick, honest voice work — not a replacement for a professional studio recording.

Why this is free

Loona Automation's founding belief is that the door in front of a tool matters as much as the tool: accounts, paywalls and trials that expire mid-job are where most people give up. Loona Voiceover is free because it runs on your device — there is no server bill for your script to pay for. The site is supported by Loona Automation and may show advertising, but using it costs nothing.

Who builds it

Loona Voiceover is part of Loona Automation, founded by Aurang Zeb Loona — a school teacher and AI teacher trainer in Jaranwala, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan — as one part of a network of 918 free tools published in 40 languages. The network's rule is simple: tools should be free, fast and open to everyone, with no sign-up wall between a person and the job they came to do.
