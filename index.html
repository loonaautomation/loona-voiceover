🤖 Loona Assistant

Awaz kaise sunun?
Script likhein, upar se voice chunein, phir Play All dabayein. Sirf ek line sunni ho to neeche us line par tap karein.
Urdu awaz kaise milegi?
Voice list me Urdu group dekhein. Agar Urdu voice na mile to ye aap ke phone/computer ki setting hai — device ki language settings me Urdu voice add hoti hai. English voices har device par milti hain.
Lamba script kaise parhun?
Poora script paste kar dein — neeche har line alag ban jayegi. Play All se sab ek ke baad ek chalega; jahan rukna ho Pause dabayein.
Apni awaz ki file kaise download karun?
Apni Awaz Record Karein wale hisse se record karein — har take ke sath Download ka button hoga. (Browser ki boli hui awaz file me download karna browser allow nahi karta — is liye recording hi download ka tareeqa hai.)
Video ke liye voiceover kahan use hoga?
Download ki hui recording ko Loona Edits me as a voice track add karein — ya kisi bhi video editor me laga lein.
